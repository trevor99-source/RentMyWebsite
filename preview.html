Yes. We set up basic SEO and Google Search Console, and submit your website to Google, so people searching for your business can find you.

What is an SSL certificate?

It is the security behind the padlock and the https in your web address. It keeps your visitors’ information private and builds trust. It is free with your website.

Do I pay hosting or for a domain?

No. Hosting and your domain are free while you rent. You only pay R350 a month, plus any add-ons you choose.

Is maintenance included?

Yes. Everyday maintenance is included: changes, additions and removal of items on your website, on request.

Is there a once-off setup fee?

No. Your first month is R350, the same as every other month.

Can I order only some services?

Yes. You don’t need a rental website. You can order marketing or add-ons on their own, and combine packages for a discount of 10% to 40%.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Just tell us 30 days before you want to cancel.

How do I own my website?

Pay R7,500 at any time and we transfer your domain and website to you. You own it from then on.

How do I pay?

Your payment is collected automatically from your card through PayFast. You can pay monthly, or pick 3, 6 or 12 months at once.

Who is Trevor?

A web designer and marketer who once couldn’t afford what he now builds.

I know what it feels like to be shut out.

I’m Trevor, a professional web designer and marketer. I started YourBrand for one reason: no business should be kept offline because of a price tag.

It took me more than 6 years to get here. Here is how it happened.

Talk to Trevor

The dream

Like many people, I wanted to build something of my own. A business that could grow, that could carry me and the people I care about. I believed that if I worked hard enough, the rest would sort itself out.

The wall

Then I ran into the wall that thousands of small business owners run into every day: everything costs money, and I didn’t have any.

I tried every road I could find. Every road had a price tag, and every price tag was out of reach. Branding. Advertising. Tools. And above all, a website.

The people who understood business all told me the same thing: if you want to be taken seriously, you need a professional setup. Big clients and big businesses expected it. They wanted to see a proper website before they would even start a conversation.

And a proper website cost R10,000 or more. Ten thousand rand. Back then, it might as well have been a million.

I had the skills, the ambition and the work ethic. I just couldn’t afford the door.

The silence

You know the moment when someone asks, “Do you have a website?” and you look away before you answer? That small silence says a lot. It says you are not ready yet. It says you are not quite one of them.

If you have felt that, you are exactly who I am writing this for.

The six years

I didn’t quit. Months turned into years. More than 6 years of trying, learning, falling short and starting again. I taught myself design. I taught myself marketing. I learned how businesses really win customers online, and I learned it the hard way, with no shortcuts and no safety net.

Some days I wondered if I was wasting my time. I kept going anyway.

The turning point

Somewhere in those years, the question changed. I stopped asking, “Why can’t I afford this?” and started asking, “How many other people are standing exactly where I stood?”

Because I was not the only one. Behind every small business is a story like mine: a good business, real customers out there waiting, and a website quote that made the owner close the page. People who feed their families with the work of their hands, held back online by one number.

The decision

So I stopped waiting for someone to open the door, and I decided to open it myself. I would build the thing I needed back then. A professional website, designed properly, with hosting, a domain and security included, for a price a small business can actually carry.

R350 a month. No R10,000. No debt before day one. Just a real website, built by someone who knows exactly what it costs to start from nothing.

I couldn’t afford a website. So I built a way for you to afford yours.

My promise to you

When you rent a website from YourBrand, you are not a ticket number. You are the person I used to be. I will design your website like it is my own, look after it every day, and make changes whenever you ask. When your business grows, I grow with you. And when you are ready, you can own it.

Let’s build yours.
