Renting a website

Contract. Your rental runs in 3-month terms and renews automatically every 3 months. You pay R350 a month, from your first month.

  1. You place your order and accept these terms.
  2. You pay your first payment.
  3. We design your website, host it and register and connect your domain.
  4. Your payment is collected automatically from your card, and we send you an invoice.

Included. Design, free hosting, a free domain, a free SSL certificate, basic SEO, Google Search Console setup, Google indexing submission and everyday maintenance for as long as you rent.

No hosting or domain fee. Renting covers hosting and your domain. You only pay R350 a month, plus any add-ons you choose.

Everyday maintenance. You can ask us to make changes, add items or remove items on your website at any time.

Ownership. You can claim your website at any time by paying R7,500. Once paid, we transfer your domain and website to you and you own it.

Cancelling. To cancel, tell us 30 days before you want to cancel.

Late or missed payments. We send an invoice every month. If payments stop, you have 3 months to catch up. After 3 months of non-payment, we may hand your account to a debt collection agency, and your details may be shared with credit bureaus, after we give you written notice. You can avoid this at any time by speaking to one of our admins, who will go through your options with you.

Social media marketing

Plans are billed monthly: Starter R2,950, Growth R5,400 and Pro R7,950. Each monthly price includes your social media ad spend and our management fee. A once-off configuration fee of R1,800 applies when you start. To cancel, tell us 30 days before. Book a free call and we will explain how it works before you commit.

Add-ons

Bundles and payment

Order two or more packages and get a bundle discount: 2 packages 10%, 3 packages 20%, 4 packages 30% and 5 or more packages 40%. A website, a marketing plan and each add-on count as one package each. You do not need a rental website to order other services.

You can pay monthly, or pay for 3, 6 or 12 months at once.
